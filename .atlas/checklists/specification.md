# Specification Checklist

## Metadata

- Version: 0.1.0
- Status: Draft
- Layer: Checklists
- Depends on: ADR-0001, principles.md, instructions/product.md

## Purpose

Verify that specifications are clear, traceable, and implementation-ready.

## Checklist

- [ ] Business intent is clear.
- [ ] Scope and non-goals are explicit.
- [ ] Requirements are testable.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are defined.
- [ ] Dependencies, assumptions, and constraints are documented.
- [ ] Open questions are captured instead of hidden in implementation.
- [ ] Downstream artifacts can trace back to this specification.
- [ ] The specification does not prescribe unnecessary implementation details too early.

## Completion Criteria

The specification is ready when a developer or AI agent can act on it without inventing undocumented behavior.
