# Product Instruction

## Metadata

- Version: 0.1.0
- Status: Draft
- Layer: Instructions
- Depends on: principles.md, ADR-0001

## Purpose

Guide product discovery, requirements, and acceptance criteria.

## Scope

Use for vision, PRD, SRS, epics, features, stories, tasks, and acceptance criteria.

## Instruction

- Clarify business intent before implementation begins.
- Capture user problems, constraints, scope, and success criteria.
- Make requirements testable and traceable.
- Avoid solution details until intent and acceptance criteria are clear.
- Keep product language aligned with domain language.

## Inputs

- Business vision, stakeholder needs, domain context, constraints.

## Outputs

- PRD, requirements, acceptance criteria, feature scope, open questions.

## Definition of Done

- Requirements are clear, testable, and traceable.
- Scope and non-goals are explicit.
